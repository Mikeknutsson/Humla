# Chauffeur goal status and imported periods

The gauge is red below goal and green at/above goal, with the goal centered. No large relative percentages are displayed. Position uses margin minus target in percentage points.

Period availability is derived from published, active-generation source data, not an empty manual certification registry. Source watermarks for Workify revenue, NEXT cost, TransPA personnel and Piusi fuel bound the latest shared date. Future depreciation/overhead schedules do not advance that date. The driver's latest canonical TransPA work date further bounds the displayed period. Default: latest 28 imported days; week and month-to-date choices are available if they contain the required vehicle data components. Owner preview and account links share identical tenant/person authorization.

Each vehicle needs revenue, cost, personnel, fuel, overhead and canonical vehicle-linked work time before receiving a goal assessment. Without a component the vehicle stays visible with a neutral meter and no advice. Comparison periods are tested independently. Presence and a shared source watermark establish availability only, not audited accounting completeness: the UI explicitly says preliminary and does not claim every invoice has arrived. Closed-period certification is not required to display operational data. No certifications are fabricated or backfilled.

The same rules accept weekly manual uploads and future daily uploads. No weekday or calendar month is hardcoded. Missing time, money or activity is not inferred, and economic SEK amounts and private salary settings remain excluded from chauffeur responses.
