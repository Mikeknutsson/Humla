# Transportledar-KPI: card explanations and deviation analysis

The overview and unit cards explain revenue, allocated costs, result, margin, dated revenue-weighted targets, percentage-point deviations and the matching prior-year period. Analysis is interpreted on the server in `src/lib/hub/transport-card-analysis.ts`, from authorized Hub aggregates provided by `hub_transport_leader_scorecard_v4`.

The financial report is identical to v3. Peer inputs preserve the caller's authorized assignments and selected months, independently of the selected unit/group drilldown. Canonical unit IDs are the comparison keys. Comparison is against the same unit in the same prior-year date/month selection and the median of at least two other units whose positive revenue belongs to the same business area. A unit earning revenue in multiple areas has no single peer group; zero-revenue cost classifications do not create a second peer area. The full unit cost is retained in unit comparisons.

Cost signals require at least SEK 5,000 current cost, an increase of at least 3 percentage points in cost/revenue and at least 25% relative to the baseline. Comparison revenue must reach SEK 10,000. Personnel baselines additionally require SEK 5,000 personnel cost to avoid interpreting virtually absent historical personnel costs as a meaningful benchmark. These are disclosed signal thresholds, not fabricated business targets. Saved margin targets determine whether a card is below goal. Missing or incomplete targets are explicitly labelled; a met target does not generate warning noise. At most two indicators are shown per card, with quantitative evidence.

The time signal requires at least 40 hours for every compared unit and at least 20% lower revenue/hour. It uses distinct TransPA report references already linked to canonical units in published personnel facts. A report assigned to multiple units or areas is ambiguous and suppresses the affected time comparison. Missing time, negative time, or other personnel sources also suppress it. No salary-derived hours, guessed report splits or double counting of repeated financial lines is permitted. NEXT-only/combined time is not treated as complete TransPA time. The UI names this limited time basis and does not call the result billable driving hours.

Area cards compare their own aggregate history and surface relevant unit indicators. The UI makes no causal claim: assignment mix, non-recurring repairs and source coverage may affect the result. Definitions and evidence criteria are available in each card. Financial facts, target settings, allocations and payment forecasts are unchanged.

Validation: `node --experimental-strip-types scripts/test-transport-card-analysis.mjs` covers materiality, targets, insufficient peers/time, prior history, excluding self/other areas and immutable financial inputs. `supabase/tests/transport_card_analysis_v4.sql` verifies exact financial parity, month/unit/group filters, retained peer scope and unauthenticated/nonmember denial. The optimized current-period RPC measured 193 ms database execution on 2026-10-28; this is a point measurement, not a service guarantee.
