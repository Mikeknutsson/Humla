# Supplier cost allocation

Effective 2025-09-01. The existing Hub publication workflow uses the saw supplier branches and replaces their prepared projections with a 50/25/25 allocation. Positive monthly non-material revenue, active economic units and a confirmed truck or tractor identify eligible Transport recipients. Credits preserve their sign. Vehicle shares differ by at most one öre per source line. Each source line must reconcile before replacement. The existing dated overhead exclusion for the Saw unit stays in effect.

The read-only rehearsal used published September 2025 through September 2026 transactions. Its 330 unique rows reconcile to the original SEK 913,022.77. Production acceptance requires applying the migration, a successful authorized report publication, a repeated-run comparison and a foreman report check.
