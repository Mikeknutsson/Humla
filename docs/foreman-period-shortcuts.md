# Foreman periods and revenue-only economic units

`/kpi/mina-enheter` defaults to fiscal YTD (September 1 through today's Stockholm date). A compact control offers YTD, latest imported revenue date, full fiscal year and individual September–August months. Fiscal year and period survive switching profiles. An opened profile uses a small row of profile links instead of the large landing cards. Unit drilldowns receive the resolved dates and selected authorized user.

`hub_foreman_revenue_cutoff_v1` returns only aggregate import-date metadata. Active supervisors and KPI readers may request their own tenant's cutoff. It considers valid, nonzero imported revenue rows from completed/needs-review batches, never failed batches or invalid rows, and caps at today's date. Both revenue and cost are queried using the same inclusive cutoff. Missing metadata disables the shortcut; explicitly requesting it without data issues no financial query. Month boundaries, YTD, fiscal-year transitions, leap years, missing data and profile query preservation are tested by `scripts/test-foreman-period.mjs`.

The economic-unit projection and table show positive-revenue units only. Direct source costs remain in the overall financial totals even when the corresponding unit is hidden. Equal shared-cost allocation and supplemental overhead use positive net monthly revenue, excluding facts marked outside KPI scope. The allocation planner now materializes eligible monthly revenue once rather than repeatedly scanning revenue for every source/recipient pair. On the report generation published 2026-10-29, verification found zero equal-allocation rows assigned to units without positive monthly revenue.

Owner-approved group defaults are Stena 6%, Bygg & Anläggning 10%, Fasta uppdrag 10%, Kranbilar 10%, Traktorer 10%, Sågning 10%, Inhyrda lastbilar 6%. Explicit dated unit targets retain precedence. Defaults are stored privately with actor, timestamp and reason; the published scorecard applies them in the backend. Återvinning was renamed Trading Material in the source group, classification labels and active prepared report; caches were invalidated and monetary totals checked unchanged.

The Ett strå vassare rule was already 75% Saw / 25% KST 10. It was checked again against the newly published generation: all supplier Saw branches equal the individually rounded 75% source amount.
