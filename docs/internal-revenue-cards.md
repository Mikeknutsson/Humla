# Internal revenue cards

Overview cards previously reused bookkeeping export readiness. An exported row
whose retained snapshot differed from current canonical receiver IDs, categories,
quantity or amount was labelled changed and excluded from the cards. This made
identity maintenance and export validation change apparent financial revenue.
The cards also read raw article categories instead of published material splits.

The cards now read the active published KPI generation. Only Workify rows with
customer Elleholms Maskin AB and status Invoiced enter their totals. Published
material/transport components retain their amounts and category; credits retain
their signs. Report period, cost-centre and other filters follow the published
financial facts. Unclassified invoices remain in the total and are flagged.
Other statuses are reported separately as not yet invoiced.

No financial facts, original imports, export snapshots or posting records change.
Bookkeeping exports keep their existing conflict checks, including real amount
or quantity changes. Card amounts are already part of KPI revenue, not extra
external turnover or expected bank receipts. The percentages use the full KPI
revenue of the same scope as denominator.
